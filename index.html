What the crop cost to protect and feed

Two tools that turn a supplier invoice and a spray log into the cost of what actually went on the crop. Start with the invoice, then read the season.

First

Invoice Normaliser

Turns a supplier invoice into a price per base unit, with every line matched to a name from the MPS catalogue so the spray log can find it.

Open the Normaliser › Then

GreenLedger

Reads the price list against one or more seasons of spray log and shows where the money went, by product, period, target and MIND class.

Open GreenLedger ›

Both tools run entirely in this browser. Invoices and spray logs are read on the device and never uploaded anywhere, so a grower's figures stay on the grower's machine.
